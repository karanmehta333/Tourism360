About Tourism 360

A modern digital initiative designed to connect travelers with hidden Himalayan destinations, authentic homestays, local culinary culture, and vital safety information in one accessible platform.

The Vision

Why We Built Tourism 360

Tourism 360 was created as an academic student innovation project aiming to solve real-world challenges faced by travelers visiting Uttarakhand. While popular hill stations often suffer from acute overcrowding and commercial strain, remote regions rich in culture, scenic grandeur, and organic agricultural heritage remain largely undiscovered.

Our mission is encapsulated in our core philosophy: “Explore More. Experience More.” We believe tourism should empower local communities, respect delicate mountain ecosystems, and provide every traveler with authentic, safe, and fulfilling memories.

The Challenge

Scattered travel info, lack of unified emergency contact resources, difficulty in locating verified village homestays, and heavy overcrowding in a handful of well-known towns.

The Solution

A clean, ultra-responsive digital hub combining curated region guides, an interactive vanilla JS itinerary generator, verified local cuisine spotlights, and quick-access emergency support.

Project Pillars

Key Innovations & Benefits

⚡

Zero-Bloat Vanilla Tech

Engineered using pure standard HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript without heavy frameworks. Loads instantly even on low-bandwidth 2G/3G mountain connections.

🏔️

Promoting Offbeat Tourism

Shines a spotlight on untouched destinations like Munsiyari, Dharchula, and Bageshwar to distribute tourism footfall equitably across Kumaon.

🤝

Community Empowerment

Connects travelers directly with local host families, certified local mountain guides, and traditional handloom weavers to retain economic value in villages.

Future Scope & Enhancements

In future versions, we plan to implement full Offline Progressive Web App (PWA) caching for trail maps, integrated real-time mountain weather alerts, and verified community review submissions.

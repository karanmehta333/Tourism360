⏱️ 1-2 Days 🏕️ High Camp Options ❄️ Snow Trekking

Soar like a Himalayan eagle with certified pilots over emerald nine-cornered lakes, pine ridges, and terraced mountain valleys. Includes GoPro HD flight video.

Chauli Ki Jali Rock Climbing

⏱️ 2 - 3 Hours 👨‍🏫 IMF Certified Guide 🪢 Rappelling

Perched in the picturesque Soar Valley, Pithoragarh offers historic Gorkha forts, serene temples like Mostamanu, limestone caves, and uninterrupted Himalayan views.

Larger than Nainital, Bhimtal is distinguished by a peaceful island in the center with an aquarium. Known for quiet kayaking, pine trails, and the historic Victoria Dam.

The cultural heart of Kumaon situated on a horse-saddle ridge. Famous for Kasar Devi's geomagnetic field, 9th-century Katarmal Sun Temple, and mouth-watering Bal Mithai.

About & Why Visit Almora

🌸 Heart of Almora: The iconic 'I Love Almora' landmark framed by blooming flowers and traditional folk murals Almora Town

Founded in 1568 by the Chand Dynasty, Almora is built upon a 5-kilometer mountain ridge surrounded by dense pine and fir forests. It was chosen as a haven of contemplation by luminaries like Swami Vivekananda, Rabindranath Tagore, and D.H. Lawrence due to its unique peace and magnetic energy.

Why Almora is Extraordinary:

  • Kasar Devi's Crank's Ridge shares a rare Van Allen radiation belt anomaly with Stonehenge and Machu Picchu.
  • Marvel at the 9th-century Katarmal Sun Temple, the second largest solar shrine in India.
  • Stroll through Lala Bazaar, a 200-year-old stone-cobbled street free from vehicular noise.
  • Watch the Himalayan alpenglow paint Trishul and Nanda Devi peaks at Bright End Corner.

Major Attractions in Almora

🕉️ Kasar Devi Temple

Kasar Devi & Crank’s Ridge

An ancient 2nd-century cave temple perched on a rocky cliff overlooking Hawalbagh valley. Famous worldwide for its peaceful aura, meditation caves, and bohemian history.

☀️ Katarmal Sun Temple

Katarmal Sun Temple (Bara Aditya)

A protected 9th-century architectural masterpiece built by Katyuri kings comprising a main sanctum and 44 carved miniature shrines perched above the Kosi River.

🌅 Bright End Corner

Bright End Corner Sunset Point

Named after Mr. Bright, this hairpin ridge vantage point provides front-row seats to spectacular sunrises and sunsets illuminating the snow peaks.

🛍️ Lala Bazaar

Lala Bazaar & Heritage Street

A historic stone-paved traditional marketplace famed for authentic Kumaoni Bal Mithai, Singori, copperware, and handloom tweed garments.

Nature Trails & Day Trips

🌲 Binsar Sanctuary & Zero Point

A 30km day excursion through dense oak canopy leading to Zero Point for an unmatched 300km Himalayan snow panorama.

🛕 Jageshwar Dham Complex

Located 35km away, explore 124 ancient stone temples nestled inside towering thousand-year-old deodar forests.

Culinary Heritage of Almora

Almora is the birthplace of Bal Mithai (caramelized roasted khoya fudge coated in sugar balls) and Singori (sweet khoya wrapped in fragrant Malu leaves). Savor a lunch of Kafuli (whipped spinach and fenugreek gravy), Gahat Ki Dal, and crispy Aloo Ke Gutke.

About & Why Visit Bhimtal

🛶 Emerald Waters of Bhimtal: Serene lake reflections surrounded by verdant pine and oak forested hills Bhimtal Lake

Named after the Pandava prince Bhima of Mahabharata legend, Bhimtal is the largest lake in the Kumaon region. Unlike its bustling neighbor Nainital, Bhimtal preserves an easy-going pace, clean pine-scented air, and tranquil waterfronts where visitors can truly decompress.

Highlights of Bhimtal:

  • Take a colorful boat to the quaint island in the middle of the lake housing a charming aquarium.
  • Visit the iconic Victoria Dam built in 1883 surrounded by cascading stepped terraced gardens.
  • Explore the Butterfly Research Centre, housing India's fourth largest collection of Lepidoptera.
  • Hike to Hidimba Parvat, a serene forested hill with historical cave hermitages.

Major Attractions in Bhimtal

🏝️ Bhimtal Island

Bhimtal Island & Aquarium

A picturesque natural island located 91 meters from the shore, accessible only by rowboat, housing an aquarium with exotic fish species from Mexico, South Africa, and India.

🏛️ Victoria Dam

Victoria Dam & Botanical Terraces

A 40-foot high stone masonry dam built in 1883 that holds the waters of Bhimtal, flanked by terraced flower gardens and peaceful walking paths.

🦋 Butterfly Centre

Butterfly Research Centre & Museum

Curated in Fred Smetacek's colonial estate, displaying over 2,400 species of rare butterflies, moths, beetles, and spiders from across the Himalayas.

🌲 Hidimba Parvat

Hidimba Parvat & Vankhandi Ashram

A scenic 5km forest hill trek through chir pines named after the mythological demoness Hidimba, culminating at an environmental conservation ashram.

Outdoor & Water Adventures

🛶 Kayaking & Paddle Boating

Glide peacefully through the calm waters of Bhimtal or nearby Naukuchiatal with certified equipment.

🪂 Paragliding at Naukuchiatal

Located 4km away, experience tandem flights soaring over nine-cornered lakes and emerald valleys.

Local Food & Culture

Relish fresh local lake fish preparations, Bhatt Ke Dubke, and home-style Kumaoni Thalis prepared with locally grown herbs like Jakhya and Gandherani. Enjoy herbal teas infused with wild cinnamon and lemongrass overlooking the lake.

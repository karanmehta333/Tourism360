About & Why Visit Bageshwar

🛕 Lord of the Sacred Confluence: Magnificent statue of Lord Shiva overlooking the sacred river ghats and Bagnath town Saryu River Ghats, Bageshwar

Known as the 'Varanasi of the Hills', Bageshwar (Vyaghreshwar - 'Lord of Tigers') is considered holy ground where sage Markandeya lived and worshipped Lord Shiva. Flanked by the mountains of Nileshwar and Bhileshwar, it connects ancient stone temple heritage with high-altitude glacier adventures into the Great Himalayan range.

🌟 Highlights of Bageshwar:

  • Pay homage at the 1450 AD Bagnath stone temple right at the sacred river confluence.
  • Visit the iconic 12th-century Baijnath Temple complex on the emerald Gomati riverbanks.
  • Basecamp for legendary treks to Pindari Glacier (Zero Point 3,660m) and Kafni Glacier.
  • Witness Uttarayani Mela, one of Uttarakhand's oldest and most vibrant cultural fairs in January.

Major Attractions in Bageshwar

🛕 Bagnath Temple

Bagnath Temple & River Sangam

Constructed in 1450 AD by Chand King Laxmi Chand at the confluence of Saryu and Gomati, housing intricately sculpted stone idols dating back to the 7th century.

🏛️ Baijnath Temple

Baijnath Ancient Temple Complex (22 km)

A protected cluster of 12th-century stone shrines dedicated to Goddess Parvati and Lord Shiva, situated by an artificial lake populated with sacred Golden Mahseer fish.

🧊 Pindari Glacier Base

Pindari & Kafni Glacier Base

The staging hub where trekkers gear up to traverse lush rhododendron valleys, alpine bugyals of Khati and Dwali, reaching the snout of the majestic Pindari Glacier.

🌿 Kanda Tea Valley

Kanda Village & Organic Tea Estates

A scenic rural township (26km away) famous for terraced organic tea gardens, traditional brass metalcraft workshops, and welcoming village homestays.

Glacier & Ridge Treks

🥾 Pindari Glacier Trek

A classic 6-day beginner-friendly glacier trek (90km round-trip) culminating at Zero Point (3,660m) under Nanda Devi Sanctuary.

🌲 Vijaypur Pine Ridge Hike

A peaceful ridge hike (30km away) blanketed in deep pine forests offering uninterrupted panoramic views of snow-capped Trishul.

Local Food & Confluence Traditions

Bageshwar’s local staples include Jhangora Ki Kheer (barnyard millet dessert sweetened with mountain jaggery), Bhatt Ke Chulche, and crispy Pahadi Arsa and Rotana festive delicacies.

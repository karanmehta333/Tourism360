About & Why Visit Mukteshwar

🍎 Snow Panorama & Orchards: 180° front-row vista of the Great Himalayan snow peaks Mukteshwar Dham & Chauli Ki Jali

Mukteshwar derives its name from a 350-year-old temple of Lord Shiva (Mukteshwar Dham), referring to the deity who grants salvation (Mukti). Surrounded by coniferous forests, oak groves, and sprawling apple and apricot orchards, it offers one of the clearest and closest viewpoints of Nanda Devi, Trishul, and Nanda Ghunti.

Highlights of Mukteshwar:

  • Watch golden sunrise alpenglow illuminate Nanda Devi (India's second highest peak).
  • Experience rock climbing, rappelling, and ziplining over deep valley gorges at Chauli Ki Jali.
  • Hike to Bhalu Gaad waterfall, a hidden 60-foot cascading mountain pool.
  • Pluck fresh apples, plums, and peaches in season across quiet village orchards.

Top Attractions in Mukteshwar

🪨 Chauli Ki Jali

Chauli Ki Jali (Lover's Rock)

A natural lattice of overhanging rocky cliffs offering dizzying vertical drops, breathtaking valley views, and ideal spots for adventure rock sports and sunset viewing.

🛕 Mukteshwar Dham

Mukteshwar Dham Shiva Temple

An ancient 350-year-old stone sanctuary located at the highest point of the town, featuring a white marble Shiva lingam and a brass idol of Nandi bull.

🌊 Bhalu Gaad Waterfall

Bhalu Gaad Hidden Waterfall

A 2km picturesque forest trail crossing rustic wooden stream bridges leading to a secluded 60-foot waterfall that flows into a natural crystal-clear pool.

🏛️ Colonial IVRI Estate

IVRI Heritage Campus & Pine Trails

Established in 1893, this historic British-era institute features sprawling pine forest trails, historic libraries, and preserved colonial architecture.

Adventure & Mountain Sports

🧗 Cliff Rappelling & Ziplining

Descend vertical rock faces at Chauli Ki Jali under certified Himalayan mountaineering instructors.

🚶 Peora Eco-Village Walk

An idyllic 10km village trail famous for organic fruit farming, traditional stone houses, and herbal crafts.

Local Food & Orchard Specialties

Indulge in artisanal organic apple, apricot, and plum preserves made by local cooperatives. Sample authentic Gahat Ka Ras (horse-gram soup), Mandua Rotis with fresh ghee, and refreshing cups of Buransh (Rhododendron) squash.

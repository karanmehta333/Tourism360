Support Local Mountain Communities

Every journey with Tourism 360 aims to channel economic value directly to local villagers, certified mountain guides, women-led handloom weavers, and organic farmers across Uttarakhand.

🧤 Handcrafted Woolens

Support Munsiyari and Dharchula tribal weavers crafting pure sheep-wool shawls.

🍯 Organic Mountain Produce

Buy raw Himalayan multiflora honey, hemp seeds, and local pulses directly.

🌱

“Leave Only Footprints”

  • Never discard single-use plastic on trekking routes.
  • Conserve water in high-altitude dry zones.
  • Respect local cultural traditions and temple customs.
  • Stay on marked eco-trails to prevent soil erosion.

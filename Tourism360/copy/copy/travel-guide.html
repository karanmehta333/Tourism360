Uttarakhand Travel Guide

Everything you need for a smooth, safe, and memorable journey through Kumaon: transit routes, packing checklists, mountain road etiquette, high-altitude advice, and eco-friendly practices.

How to Reach

Connectivity & Transportation

Reaching Uttarakhand’s hill towns is seamless by road, train, and flight connections from New Delhi and major cities.

🚆

By Train

Kathgodam Railway Station is the primary railhead for Nainital (34km), Bhimtal (30km), Almora (88km), Mukteshwar (62km), and Munsiyari (275km). Direct daily express trains connect from Delhi, Lucknow, and Kolkata.

✈️

By Air

Pantnagar Airport (PGH) is the closest domestic airport (70km from Nainital) with scheduled flights from Delhi and Dehradun. Naini Saini Airport in Pithoragarh connects regional feeder flights.

🚌

By Road

Well-paved National Highways (NH 9 and NH 109) connect Delhi to Haldwani and Almora. Regular UTC state buses, Volvo coaches, and private shared jeeps operate round-the-clock from Anand Vihar ISBT, Delhi.

Practical Advice

Mountain Safety, Packing & Etiquette

🎒

What to Pack for Uttarakhand

  • Layered Clothing: Thermal innerwear, fleece jackets, and windproof outer shells. Even in summer, evenings dip below 12°C in high ridges.
  • Trekking Footwear: Sturdy water-resistant hiking shoes with deep rubber treads.
  • Sun Protection: High UV indices at 2,000m+ require SPF 50+ sunscreen, UV sunglasses, and broad sun hats.
  • Power Backup & Offline Maps: Power banks (10,000mAh+) and downloaded offline GPS maps as mobile coverage can be patchy in deep valleys.
  • Basic First Aid: Motion sickness tablets (Avomine), Paracetamol, Band-aids, ORS hydration salts, and personal medication.
🚗

Mountain Driving Rules & Road Etiquette

  • Uphill Right of Way: Always yield and give priority to vehicles driving uphill on narrow ghat sections.
  • Engine Braking: Use lower gears (2nd or 3rd) when driving downhill to prevent brake pad overheating.
  • Avoid Night Driving: Plan to reach your destination before sunset (5:30 PM - 6:30 PM) to avoid dense mountain fog and unlit curves.
  • Honk at Blind Curves: Sound a short horn tap before navigating sharp hairpin turns.
🫁

Altitude Acclimatization (Munsiyari & High Treks)

When visiting altitudes above 2,200 meters (like Munsiyari, Khaliya Top, or Pindari Glacier), drink at least 3-4 liters of water daily. Avoid alcohol on the first 24 hours of arrival, walk at an easy rhythmic pace, and do not rush uphill climbs.

🌱

Leave Only Footprints

Uttarakhand's Himalayan ecosystems are fragile. Help us preserve them for future generations:

  • Carry all plastic wrappers and empty bottles back to towns for proper recycling.
  • Avoid loud music in pristine forest zones to protect wildlife.
  • Remove footwear outside temples and dress respectfully.
  • Support local guides and buy authentic village produce directly.

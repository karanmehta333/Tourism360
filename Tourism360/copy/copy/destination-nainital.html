About & Why Visit Nainital

🌙 Night Magic in Nainital: Serene evening view of Naini Lake dotted with traditional wooden boats and dazzling hill lights Naini Lake Promenade

According to Hindu mythology, Nainital is one of the 51 Shakti Peethas, where the emerald eye (Nain) of Goddess Sati fell, creating the sacred water body. In the 1840s, it evolved into a beloved summer retreat with charming Victorian cottages, historic schools, and classic lakeside promenades.

Why You Will Love Nainital:

  • Sail in traditional painted rowboats or modern yachts across glistening green waters.
  • Ride the aerial ropeway to Snow View for 360° panoramas of Nanda Devi and Trishul peaks.
  • Walk through the misty Kilbury oak forests, a paradise for bird enthusiasts with over 250 species.
  • Savor hot roasted corn (Bhutta), piping steamed momos, and traditional Kumaoni Bal Mithai.

Top Attractions in Nainital

🛶 Naini Lake & Boating

Naini Lake & The Promenade

The beating heart of town. Rent a paddleboat or vintage rowboat with local boatmen, stroll along Mall Road in the evening when lakeside lights shimmer over the water.

🚡 Snow View Point

Snow View Point (2,270 m)

Accessible by cable car from Mallital, offering dramatic vistas of the Great Himalayan range including Nanda Devi, Trishul, and Nanda Kot.

🛕 Naina Devi Temple

Maa Naina Devi Shrine & Tibetan Market

An ancient revered temple situated on the northern shore of Naini Lake, adjacent to the bustling Tibetan Market known for woolens and street delicacies.

🪨 Eco Cave Gardens

Eco Cave Gardens & Tiffin Top (Dorothy's Seat)

A cluster of 6 interconnected natural animal caves to crawl through, paired with a rewarding 4km pine hike up to Dorothy’s Seat for bird's-eye views.

Adventure & Outdoor Activities

🥾 Naina Peak (Cheena Peak) Trek

A 6km forest trek to Nainital’s highest elevation (2,615m) winding through deodar, cypress, and rhododendrons.

🦅 Pangot Bird Watching

Located 15km away, Pangot offers guided bird trails spotting cheer pheasants, lammergeiers, and forktails.

Local Food & Culture

Do not leave without trying Aloo Ke Gutke (spiced mountain potatoes with mustard seeds and mountain cumin), accompanied by fresh Bhang Ki Chutney and Bhatt Ki Churkani. For sweets, grab authentic Bal Mithai and Singori from heritage sweetmakers in Mallital.

Nearby Excursions

  • Bhimtal (22 km): Serene lake with a central island aquarium.
  • Sattal (24 km): An untouched cluster of seven interconnected freshwater lakes.
  • Mukteshwar (50 km): 180-degree Himalayan snow views and fruit orchards.

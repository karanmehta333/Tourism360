About & Why Visit Pithoragarh

🏔️ The Little Kashmir of Kumaon: Vast panoramic vista of Soar Valley framed by lush green hills Soar Valley, Pithoragarh

Pithoragarh is the easternmost district town of Uttarakhand, flanked by Nepal to the east and Tibet to the north. Set amidst the picturesque Soar Valley, it offers an unspoiled blend of martial history, rich Kumaoni folklore, limestone speleology, and sweeping mountain panoramas of Nanda Devi, Panchachuli, and Trishul.

Why You Will Love Pithoragarh:

  • Stunning 360-degree views of the lush green Soar Valley from Chandak Hill.
  • Visit the iconic 18th-century Pithoragarh Fort built during the Chand and Gorkha eras.
  • Explore natural limestone cave formations at Kapileshwar and Rai Gufa.
  • Gateway to high-altitude Himalayan pilgrimages including Kailash-Mansarovar & Om Parvat routes.

Major Attractions in Pithoragarh

🏰 Pithoragarh Fort

Pithoragarh Fort (London Fort)

Constructed in 1789 by the Gorkhas, this hill fortress perches high above the town, providing commanding views of the entire valley basin and snow-crested horizons.

🛕 Mostamanu Temple

Mostamanu Temple & Chandak Hills

A revered local deity shrine situated 6km away atop Chandak ridge, famous for its grand autumn fair, magnesite quarries, and peaceful sunset vistas.

🪨 Kapileshwar Mahadev

Kapileshwar Mahadev Cave Shrine

Located in the outskirts of town, this sacred Shiva shrine is nestled inside a 10-meter deep natural limestone cave where sage Kapila is believed to have meditated.

🦌 Askot Sanctuary

Askot Musk Deer Sanctuary

Spanning sub-alpine elevations (54km away), this ecological reserve safeguards the endangered Himalayan musk deer, snow leopards, and rare monal pheasants.

Adventure & Trekking Trails

🥾 Thal Kedar Ridge Hike

An 8km scenic forest trek to 8,000 feet leading to an ancient Lord Shiva sanctuary nestled amidst deodars.

🌲 Dhwaj Temple Nature Trail

A tranquil hike through dense pine and oak woods rewarding trekkers with unobstructed sights of Mt. Trishul.

Local Food & Culture

The cuisine of Pithoragarh is deeply grounded in mountain staples. Try Bhatt Ki Churkani (black soy bean gravy slow-simmered in an iron kadhai), Dubuk served with warm Jhangora rice, and Mandua Ki Roti spread with fresh mountain butter. Don't miss traditional Hiljatra mask-dance festivals during harvest season.

Nearby Places to Explore

  • Munsiyari (128 km): Basecamp for Panchachuli peaks and alpine bugyals.
  • Dharchula (92 km): Indo-Nepal border town on the Kali River.
  • Gangolihat (77 km): Home to the famed Hat Kalika Shakti Peeth temple and Patal Bhuvaneshwar caves.
